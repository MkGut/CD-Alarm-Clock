CD Alarm Clock!
What it does:
This is a traditional alarm clock with a spin on it! Quite literally!! This alarm clock plays your favorite loud music to jolt you awake as soon as your alarm is triggered, and makes sure that you turn down the volume of your blaring alarm. But how you may be asking? And I say to that, by stopping the CD disc with your hand. As soon as the spinning disc stops, the volume will turn down to a much more manageable volume, and you can start pausing, choosing songs, andT setting alarms. The project also functions as a music player as well, and when in music player mode, the music can be stopped/paused by stopping the disc instead of pressing the rotary encoder button. I hope you enjoy!

How it works:
The alarm clock primarily runs on am ESP32 C3, which forms the brains of the operation. The esp32 C3 has wifi, which is used to pull time and date. The music neds to be downloaded as week, however, in the future, it could possibly be updated to be used as a voice based ststem that grab songs from online.
The hardware generally works like this. Say you set an alarm for 7. The ESP checks the time repeatedly, pulling from the wifi, and once it determines its 7 AM, it picks a random song from the SD card and plays it at max volume to wake you up. At the same time, it turns on the motor connected to the disc n order to have the user be able to turn something off.
