CD Alarm Clock!
What it does:
This is a traditional alarm clock with a spin on it! Quite literally!! This alarm clock plays your favorite loud music to jolt you awake as soon as your alarm is triggered, and makes sure that you turn down the volume of your blaring alarm. But how you may be asking? And I say to that, by stopping the CD disc with your hand. As soon as the spinning disc stops, the volume will turn down to a much more manageable volume, and you can start pausing, choosing songs, and setting alarms. The project also functions as a music player as well, and when in music player mode, the music can be stopped/paused by stopping the disc instead of pressing the rotary encoder button. I hope you enjoy!
